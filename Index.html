About Me

Hi, I’m Jay, photographer based in Croatia.

My work explores the bold, the intimate, and everything in between—from spontaneous portraits to raw, sensual moments and stylized nudes that blur the line between vulnerability and strength.

Every session is a collaboration. Whether it’s a conceptual shoot, a boudoir session, or a spontaneous portrait, I aim to create a space where self-expression is celebrated and boundaries are reimagined.

Let’s make something unforgettable.
